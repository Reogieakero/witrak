Permission model per ROLES-AND-MODULES.md §2 — Fees: view, create & verify = Super Admin and Treasurer. Secretary, Discipline Officer, and Year Rep have no fees surface. Flow per DESIGN.md §9: the Treasurer creates a Fee (faculty-wide, no scope in Rev. 2), the student uploads a proof of payment (status pending), and the fees.verify_payment holder approves or rejects — rejection requires a reason. Status becomes paid or rejected; every verification is audit-logged (fee.payment_verified / fee.payment_rejected). On the student surface (ROLES-AND-MODULES.md §3), each student sees the posted fees and uploads their own proof of payment — view-own only, no directory, no verification controls.
